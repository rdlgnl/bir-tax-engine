# BuwisBuddy — tax rule review for a CPA

**Purpose.** BuwisBuddy is an open-source desktop app that helps Filipino
freelancers and sole proprietors keep records, compute their taxes and fill
the BIR's own forms. Every rate, threshold, deadline and form it uses was
encoded by the developers from the NIRC and BIR issuances, **without
professional review**, and the app was released that way, telling its users
so. This sheet lists each rule exactly as the app applies it, so any CPA or
tax lawyer can confirm or correct it. Send what you find as an issue or pull
request (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)); a rule you sign off is
recorded with your name and shown as reviewed in the app.

**How to review.** For each row, write in **Decision**:

- **OK**: correct as applied, for the periods shown;
- **Change**: what it should be, with the basis;
- **N/A**: the app shouldn't apply it at all.

Use **Notes** for conditions, effective dates or issuances we missed. The
**Basis** column is the developers' understanding, not a verified citation;
please correct it where it's wrong or incomplete. The
section **Questions** at the end needs a short written answer each.

**Worked returns.** [REFERENCE-CASES.md](REFERENCE-CASES.md) shows these
rules at work: ten typical taxpayers and the returns the app prepares for
them, line by line. Please check those figures with this sheet.

**Scope.** Individuals (self-employed or mixed income, 8% or graduated
rates, VAT or non-VAT), the taxpayer as a withholding agent and employer,
documentary stamp tax on loans, and one corporation the taxpayer runs.
Periods from **January 1, 2018** (corporations from **January 1, 2021**);
earlier periods are refused by the app.

Rule pack version under review: **2026.11** ([config/tax.php](../config/tax.php), with its parts in [resources/rule-pack/](../resources/rule-pack/)). Each part can be signed off on its own under `parts` in config/tax.php.
Reference letters such as `IT-GRAD-2023` are the app's own rule ids.
