Why don't I pay percentage tax?
Because you chose the flat 8% option on your Q1 return. The 8% covers both your income tax and the 3% percentage tax, so there's no separate quarterly percentage tax return for you this year. One loose end: your COR still lists percentage tax. Updating it (Form 1905) keeps the BIR from expecting 2551Q returns.
